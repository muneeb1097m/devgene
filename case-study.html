Live Team Roping

Project Overview

Live Team Roping is a cutting-edge real-time multiplayer mobile simulation that captures the authentic spirit and technical precision of professional team roping. Developed in Unity, the project focused on delivering frame-perfect physics and a seamless competitive environment for players worldwide.

The Objective

Our primary goal was to bridge the gap between casual arcade gameplay and high-fidelity simulation. We needed to solve for complex rope physics, horse-rider synchronization, and sub-100ms latency in multiplayer interactions to ensure a "fair play" environment.

Core Systems

Multiplayer Sync

Proprietary netcode solution for handling high-frequency physics updates without jitter.

Custom Physics

Verlet integration based rope system for realistic lasso behavior and tension.

AI Integration

Neural-network driven steer behavior for unpredictable and challenging AI matches.

Asset Pipeline

Automated character customization pipeline supporting thousands of unique gear combinations.

The Challenge

The biggest hurdle was the synchronization of the "catch" moment. In team roping, a fraction of a second determines success. We implemented a client-side prediction model with server-side validation that maintains the feeling of responsiveness while preventing cheating.

The Result

3M+ Sessions. 99.9% Server Uptime. Top 50 in Sports Category.

The project successfully scaled from a prototype to a full live-ops title, proving DevGene's ability to handle complex Unity systems and high-traffic multiplayer environments.
